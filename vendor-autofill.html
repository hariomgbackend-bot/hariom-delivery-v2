Hariom Vendor Autofill

Fills the Haier and Bajaj service-request forms from a service ticket. Open the form from a ticket in the Service Panel (the ticket's details travel in the link), then tap the Hariom Autofill bookmarklet on that page — it fills the form. OTP, captcha and Submit always stay manual by a human.

Install once (desktop)

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome/Edge).
  2. Drag the green Hariom Autofill button below onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. Done. On any Haier/Bajaj form you open from a ticket, click it once to fill.
⚡ Hariom Autofill
Drag this to your bookmarks bar — or use the copy buttons below.
Can't drag? Copy the script and paste it into the page's developer console (F12 → Console) instead. Or add a normal bookmark, then edit it and paste the copied bookmarklet into the URL field.
iPhone/iPad: bookmarklets work in Safari, but installing them means adding a bookmark and editing its address. 

  
Internal use only. OTP and final submission must always be completed manually by a human.
